  2. 2

Frequently asked questions

Is Group Contacts Exporter free to use?

Yes — install and export with no sign-up and no card required today. Pricing may evolve as we add more advanced features, but the basic export flow is free to use right now.

Does it work with large WhatsApp groups?

Yes — it handles groups with hundreds or even thousands of members.

Will every member have a phone number in the export?

WhatsApp doesn't show a phone number for every member. Those members export with their name and a blank number, and the extension tells you exactly how many after each export — so you're never guessing.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Extraction happens entirely inside your browser. Nothing about your contacts is uploaded, stored, or sold.

Can the extension send messages or add contacts?

No. It only reads groups you're already in. It never sends messages, adds contacts, or automates anything.

Is this affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta?

No. Group Contacts Exporter is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to WhatsApp LLC, Meta Platforms Inc., or any Meta product. WhatsApp™ is a trademark of WhatsApp LLC, referenced here solely to describe compatibility.

Do I need to use the browser console or paste any code?

No. A lot of the "free" guides for this floating around involve opening DevTools and pasting a JavaScript snippet into the console — that's not what this is. Install the extension, click the icon, click Extract. No code, no console.

Is there a limit on how many contacts I can export for free?

No hidden cap. Some tools in this space show you the first 10 contacts free and hide the rest until you pay — this exports the whole group, every time.

Can I export just admins, or exclude admins from the file?

Not as a separate filter yet — every row includes a Role column (Owner, Admin, or Member), so you can sort or filter it in Excel or Sheets after export in a few seconds.

Does this work with WhatsApp Communities?

A Community is really just several groups grouped under one umbrella — you can export any of those individual groups the same way, or tick "export all groups" to pull every group you're in, community sub-groups included, into one file.

What format does the export come in — Excel or CSV?

CSV, which opens directly in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers with no import step. If your CRM or spreadsheet tool accepts CSV (nearly all do), you're good to go.
