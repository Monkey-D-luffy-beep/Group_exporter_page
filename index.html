How it works

From WhatsApp group to spreadsheet in four clicks.

  1. 1

    Open WhatsApp Web

    Log into web.whatsapp.com like you normally would.

  2. 2

    Click the extension icon

    Group Contacts Exporter opens right from your Chrome toolbar.

  3. 3

    Pick a group

    Search and select any group — or tick "all groups" to export everything at once.

  4. 4

    Extract, then download

    Click Extract and get a clean CSV that opens straight in Excel or Google Sheets.

Frequently asked questions

Is Group Contacts Exporter free to use?

Yes. It's completely free, with no sign-up required.

Does it work with large WhatsApp groups?

Yes — it handles groups with hundreds or even thousands of members.

Will every member have a phone number in the export?

WhatsApp doesn't show a phone number for every member. Those members export with their name and a blank number, and the extension tells you exactly how many after each export — so you're never guessing.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Extraction happens entirely inside your browser. Nothing about your contacts is uploaded, stored, or sold.

Can the extension send messages or add contacts?

No. It only reads groups you're already in. It never sends messages, adds contacts, or automates anything.
